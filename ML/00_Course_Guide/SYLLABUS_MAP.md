# Syllabus map

Source: [official syllabus, PDF pages 1–2](../../syllabus_final_year_it.pdf), course IT/PC/B/T/411. The PDF presents eight blocks without explicit unit numbers; folders number these blocks in their printed order, consistent with the original ML readme.

## 1. Introduction to Machine Learning

Applications; design perspective and issues; supervised and unsupervised learning; formal learning model and representation; runtime of learning.

[Material](../01_Introduction_to_ML/README.md)

## 2. Evaluating Hypotheses and Bayesian Learning

Estimating hypothesis accuracy; sampling theory; comparing learning algorithms; Bayes theorem; concept learning; Bayes optimal classifier; naive Bayes; Bayesian belief networks.

[Material](../02_Hypothesis_Evaluation_and_Bayesian_Learning/README.md)

## 3. Artificial Neural Networks

Early models; perceptron rule; multilayer perceptrons; artificial neurons; activation functions; multilayer neural networks.

[Material](../03_Artificial_Neural_Networks/README.md)

## 4. Feedforward Neural Networks and Graphical Models

Risk minimization; loss functions; backpropagation; initialization; training and validation; regularization; model selection; optimization. Also in this syllabus block: linear-chain CRFs, partition function, Markov networks, belief propagation, training CRFs, HMMs and entropy.

[Material](../04_Feedforward_Networks_and_Graphical_Models/README.md)

## 5. Probabilistic Neural Networks

Hopfield nets; Boltzmann machines; RBMs; sigmoid nets; autoencoders.

[Material](../05_Probabilistic_Neural_Networks/README.md)

## 6. Modern Practical Deep Networks

Deep-learning perspectives and issues; deep feedforward networks; regularization; optimization; CNNs; recurrent and recursive networks.

[Material](../06_Modern_Deep_Networks/README.md)

## 7. Classification and Clustering

Linear classification; logistic regression; LDA; separating hyperplanes; SVM formulation, interpretation and analysis; nonlinearly separable data. The syllabus heading mentions clustering but does not enumerate clustering algorithms in this block.

[Material](../07_Classification_and_Clustering/README.md)

## 8. Reinforcement Learning

RL framework, methods and applications; immediate RL; bandits; value functions; policy search and REINFORCE; contextual bandits; returns and MDPs; dynamic programming; Monte Carlo; value and policy iteration; off-policy MC; UCT; TD(0); Q-learning.

[Material](../08_Reinforcement_Learning/README.md)
